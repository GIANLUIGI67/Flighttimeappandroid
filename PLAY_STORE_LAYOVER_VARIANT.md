# Google Play Layover and Crew variant

## Positioning

FlightTimeApp is the all-in-one companion for airline crews: connect during
layovers, discover cities, share aviation experiences, track salary, and keep
training current.

## Listing copy

Title (30/30):

`FlightTimeApp: Crew & Layovers`

Short description (79/80):

`Crew layovers, city guides, aviation social network, salary and training tools.`

Full description:

FlightTimeApp is the all-in-one app for airline pilots and cabin crew. Connect
with nearby crew during layovers, discover useful city information, share
aviation experiences, track monthly salary and keep recurrent training current.

CREW LAYOVERS AND NEARBY CREW

See crew members near your layover, create your crew profile and connect with
other aviation professionals. Use layover chat and crew events to organize time
together while away from base.

LAYOVER CITY INFORMATION

Explore useful information for your destination and share local tips,
recommendations and practical notes with other crew members. Keep the knowledge
of the aviation community available for future layovers.

AVIATION SOCIAL NETWORK

Join a social space created for pilots and cabin crew. Follow community updates,
share posts, participate in Crew Forum discussions and keep conversations with
aviation colleagues organized.

SALARY AND FLIGHT TRACKING

Record flights and sectors, review monthly activity and estimate salary using
contract parameters, allowances, deductions and layovers. Keep previous months
available for comparison and personal records.

PILOT AND CABIN CREW TRAINING

Practice training questions, procedures and voice memory items for supported
aircraft families:

- A320 training and memory items
- A330 training and memory items
- A380 training and memory items
- B737 training and memory items
- B787 training and memory items

ESSENTIAL CREW UTILITIES

Use flight-time calculations and other practical aviation tools when needed,
without switching between multiple apps.

WHO FLIGHTTIMEAPP IS FOR

- Airline pilots, cadet pilots and student pilots
- Cabin crew and student cabin crew
- Aviation professionals preparing recurrent training
- Crew members connecting and exploring cities during layovers

FlightTimeApp brings layovers, city knowledge, crew connections, salary,
training and practical aviation tools together in one mobile experience.
